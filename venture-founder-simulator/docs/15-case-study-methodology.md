# 15. Case Study Sourcing Methodology

## 1. Rules

1. Public, verifiable sources only: company websites, founder interviews on record, regulatory filings (MCA, DRHP/RHP), annual reports, credible journalism, published academic cases (cited, not copied).
2. Every statement in a case is tagged **Fact** (with source) or **Inference** (with reasoning). Inference is visually distinct.
3. No private details, no inferred undisclosed financials. If unit economics are not disclosed, the case says "not publicly disclosed".
4. Every case has a "current position as of [date]" block and a review-by date.
5. Cases are illustrations, not templates. Each ends with "What would be different for you" prompts.
6. Copyright: summarise in our own words; short quotations only with attribution; never reproduce paid case content.
7. Living founders: neutral tone, factual; correction requests handled through a documented process.

## 3. Selection criteria

- Mix of outcomes: scaled, steady owner-operated, pivoted, and closed. **Include failures.** A library of only winners teaches survivorship bias.
- Mix of capital paths: bootstrapped, debt-funded, VC-funded, franchise-led.
- Bengaluru and India first; selected global cases where the lesson travels.
- At least 2 cases per Tier F vertical at launch.

## 4. Candidate pool (to research; no claims made here)

Indicative names for the research team to assess against availability of public, verifiable information. Inclusion here is not a statement about any company:

- Food and beverage: Indian speciality coffee chains, tea chains, Bengaluru darshini-format restaurants, cloud-kitchen operators.
- SaaS: Indian vertical SaaS and SMB software companies with public filings.
- Salons and wellness: Indian salon chains, boutique fitness operators.
- Healthcare: dental and physiotherapy chains, diagnostics networks.
- Consumer brands and marketplaces: D2C brands with offer documents, home-services marketplaces.

Companies with public offer documents (DRHP/RHP) are the richest source of verified unit economics and should be prioritised.

## 5. Production workflow

Research (2 to 4 hours per case) → draft with fact/inference tags → source check by second person → legal/tone review for living founders → publish with review-by date → annual refresh.
