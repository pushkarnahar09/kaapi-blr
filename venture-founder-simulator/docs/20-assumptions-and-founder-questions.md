# 20. Key Assumptions and Questions Requiring Founder Input

## 1. Assumptions made in this blueprint (change any of them)

| # | Assumption | Impact if wrong |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | English only for MVP | Regional-language demand would need i18n from M2 |
| A2 | Desktop-first web app; no native mobile | Phone-first users (e.g. small business owners) underserved |
| A3 | Simulated company follows Indian GAAP (AS) with Schedule III presentation; Ind AS out of scope | Some B-schools may want Ind AS treatment for leases |
| A4 | Reference verticals: Speciality Cafe and Vertical SaaS | Changes M3/M5 scope |
| A5 | Research Packs are curated by humans, AI-drafted at most | Slower content growth, much higher trust |
| A6 | Single-player markets: players do not compete for the same simulated customers | Multiplayer competition is a large later feature |
| A7 | India-region hosting, managed Postgres | Cost and vendor choice |
| A8 | Founder (you) acts as finance and tax reviewer for content | Your time becomes a bottleneck; budget for a second reviewer |
| A9 | Time before launch advances by activity duration; daily ticks after launch | |
| A10 | Pre-launch spending is from founder's personal funds unless an entity exists | Affects how Stage 5 timing is taught |

## 2. Questions for you

### Strategy and commercial

1. **Who is the first paying customer?** Individual founders, incubators, or B-schools? My recommendation: incubators and B-schools first. They have budget, a cohort to pilot with, and a reason to pay.
2. **Is this a product you plan to commercialise, or first a tool for your own founder journey?** If it is primarily for you, the build should start with the cafe vertical and your own real fieldwork, and the education layer can wait much longer.
3. **What does success look like in 12 months?** Revenue, number of institutions, number of users, or a working personal decision?
4. **Pricing hypothesis?** Per-seat per-cohort for institutions vs subscription for individuals.

### Scope

5. Confirm the two reference verticals (Cafe + SaaS), or name the ones you want.
6. Which 12 industries should get Research Packs first?
7. Is education mode needed for a specific institution by a specific date?
8. Do you want real-world fieldwork logging (players record real interviews and counts) in MVP, or simulated only?

### Resources

9. Who writes the code, and how many hours per week?
10. Budget for paid research (industry reports, databases), legal review of compliance content, and design?
11. Who else can review tax, legal and clinical content? A second CA, a company secretary, a lawyer, a clinician advisor?

### Product principles

12. How strict should gates be? Default soft gates with logged overrides; or hard gates for B-school mode?
13. Should AI-drafted research ever be visible to players before review? (Default: no.)
14. Should the simulator show any "typical" benchmarks before we have sourced ones, or leave them blank? (Default: blank.)

### Repository

15. Create a dedicated repository for the simulator? (Recommended. The current repository is the Kaapi BLR coffee-finder PWA.)

## 3. Questions the simulator will ask players (Stage 0 script, first version)

1. Why do you want to start a business now?
2. What would make this a success for you in 3 years: income, independence, scale, impact, or a sale?
3. What have you done professionally, and which industries do you know from the inside?
4. How many hours a week can you give this for the next 6 months? Will that change?
5. How much personal capital could you put in?
6. How much of that could you lose completely without changing your life plans?
7. How do you react to uncertainty about income for 12 to 18 months? (scaled)
8. Do you want to manage people and physical operations day to day?
9. Which excites you more: technology, consumer brands, or services?
10. Do you want a co-founder? If yes, what should they be strong at?
11. Are you open to working anywhere in Bengaluru, or only certain areas? Other cities?
12. Do you want a business that pays you steadily, or one that aims for rapid growth with outside investors?
13. Does your current employment contract, professional body membership or any agreement restrict what you can do?
14. When, if ever, would you want to exit?
